**A unicorn. A rainbow. The rainbow *is* the fuel.**

Fly her across 13 stages, from a sunlit forest to an orbital station, and see how far you can get before the rainbow runs dry.

The rainbow behind her is the clock at the top of the screen, and it is the only resource in the game.

- Every ring you fly through is **+0.5 s**, doubled while the DOUBLE gem runs.
- Every hit **spills 10 s** on the ground.
- Whatever is left when you clear a stage is what you **start the next one with**.

So a stage is never really finished: the fuel you save in the forest is the fuel you'll still be burning in orbit. Stage 1 always grants a fresh 60 s, and the endless run at the end of the road is only as long as the fuel you brought.
### Controls

- **Hold** — mouse, finger, `Space`, `Enter` or `↑` — wings out, she climbs.
- **Release** — she folds up and falls.
- **`←` `→`** or swipe — browse the stage list.
- **`Esc`** — back to the list, or back to the title.

That's the whole input: one button, held or not. Everything else is timing.

### What's in it

- **13 stages** — forest, factory, candyland, seabed, desert, city, icefield,   jungle, beach, volcano, neon grid, orbital station, then an endless run.
- **13 original tunes**, one per world plus the title theme.
- **6 power-ups**: SHIELD, MAGNET, DOUBLE, TURBO, RUSH and the SUPER RING.
- Zappers, falling blocks, updraughts and homing missiles.

Not one image and not one sample: every shape is a canvas path, and the music is generated through the Web Audio API.
